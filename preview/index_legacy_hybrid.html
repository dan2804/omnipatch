`midir` handles platform audio subsystems directly without bloated C++ runtimes:

  • Windows: WinMM or WinRT MIDI
  • macOS: CoreMIDI framework
  • Linux: ALSA sequencer & raw MIDI
  • FreeBSD/OpenBSD: JACK audio or sndio layer

  param_id: u8,   // 0..127

}
